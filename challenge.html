Planning for Greater Challenge

Approaches for increasing the depth and complexity of learning without simply increasing the quantity of work.

Depth Before Acceleration

Help pupils explore ideas more deeply before moving prematurely to new or more advanced content.

Low-Floor, High-Ceiling Tasks

Design tasks that are accessible to all pupils while allowing increasingly sophisticated responses.

Productive Struggle

Create appropriate challenge while giving pupils time and support to persevere.

Removing the Thinking Ceiling

Use open questions, multiple approaches and extension prompts that deepen rather than merely lengthen tasks.

Improving the Level of Challenge in a Task

Practical ways to adapt existing classroom activities.

Require Justification

Ask pupils to explain their reasoning, provide evidence and defend their conclusions.

Introduce Constraints

Add carefully chosen conditions that require pupils to think more strategically.

Compare and Evaluate

Ask pupils to compare methods, judge effectiveness and identify the strongest approach.

Increase Independence

Reduce unnecessary scaffolding and require pupils to make more decisions about how to approach the task.
