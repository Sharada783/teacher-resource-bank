Writing

Approaches and resources for developing analytical, argumentative, creative and transactional writing.

Analytical Writing

Support pupils to develop precise claims, select relevant evidence and explain how language, structure and context contribute to meaning.

Useful prompts

What is your central interpretation?

Which evidence best supports it?

How does the evidence establish meaning?

What alternative interpretation could be considered?

Creative Writing

Develop control of viewpoint, structure, description, dialogue, sentence choices and deliberate language effects.

Try this

Ask pupils to imitate one carefully selected feature from a mentor text before applying it independently.

Grammar for Writing

Teach grammar through meaningful writing decisions, connecting grammatical knowledge to purpose, audience and effect.

Stretch, Challenge and Enrichment

Opportunities to deepen interpretation, increase independence and extend pupils beyond routine examination responses.

Comparative and Critical Reading

Ask pupils to compare interpretations, evaluate critical viewpoints and examine how different texts address related themes or ideas.

Challenge prompts

Which interpretation is most convincing?

How does the second text complicate the first?

What assumptions underpin this critical viewpoint?

Which evidence could be used to challenge it?

Independent Literary Exploration

Give pupils structured opportunities to select texts, form their own questions, research context and present sustained interpretations.

Possible outcomes

  • Comparative essays
  • Critical presentations
  • Reading journals
  • Creative transformations
  • Student-led seminars
